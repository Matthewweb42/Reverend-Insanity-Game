# Clans & Starting Factions [DRAFT]

## Overview

Clans determine starting position, initial Gu access, and early-game political landscape. Each clan has unique strengths, exclusive Gu, and distinct playstyles.

---

## Clan Structure

### What Clans Provide

- **Starting Location** - Where you begin
- **Signature Paths** - 2-3 paths with bonuses
- **Unique Gu Pool** - 5-10 exclusive Gu
- **Starting Resources** - Initial wealth and items
- **Clan Politics** - NPC relationships and standing
- **Clan Missions** - Exclusive quest lines
- **Clan Techniques** - Unique killer moves

### Clan Standing

Your position within the clan:

| Standing | Description | Benefits |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Outsider | Not a member | No clan benefits |
| Probationary | New/untrusted | Basic missions only |
| Member | Standard | Full mission access |
| Core | Valued member | Advanced missions, discounts |
| Elder | Leadership | Clan resources, authority |

**Standing affects:**
- Mission availability and rewards
- Market prices within clan
- NPC dispositions
- Access to clan secrets

## Southern Border Clans

### Gu Yue Clan

**Theme:** Moonlight and Balance

**Overview:**
The Gu Yue clan is known for their balanced cultivation approach and moonlight-based techniques. A good starting clan for learning the game.

**Signature Paths:**
- Light Path (Primary)
- Refinement Path
- Wisdom Path

**Starting Position:**
- Location: Gu Yue Village
- Age: 15
- Resources: 50 primeval stones
- Starting Gu: Basic Moonlight Gu (Rank 1)

**Unique Gu Pool:**
```
Moonlight Gu (Rank 1, Light, Offensive)
- Clan signature offensive technique

Crescent Shield Gu (Rank 2, Light, Defensive)
- Enhanced at night

Moon Phase Gu (Rank 3, Light, Utility)
- Effects change with moon cycle

Lunar Reflection Gu (Rank 3, Light/Wisdom, Defensive)
- Reflect attacks under moonlight

Full Moon Gu (Rank 4, Light, Offensive)
- Massive power during full moon
```

**Clan Bonuses:**
- +10% Light Path attainment gain
- Night-time combat bonuses
- Balanced stat distribution

**Clan Politics:**
- Neutral with most factions
- Rivals with Bai Clan
- Trade relations with merchants

**Unique Killer Move (Learnable):**
```
Moonlight Devastation
Requires: Master Light Path, 3 moonlight Gu
Effect: Massive light damage, bonus at night
```

### Bai Clan

**Theme:** Bone and Aggression

**Overview:**
The Bai clan practices aggressive bone path cultivation. Known for brutal efficiency and combat prowess. Higher risk, higher reward playstyle.

**Signature Paths:**
- Strength Path (Primary)
- Blood Path
- Transformation Path

**Starting Position:**
- Location: Bai Clan Territory
- Age: 16
- Resources: 35 primeval stones
- Starting Gu: Bone Spike Gu (Rank 1)

**Unique Gu Pool:**
```
Bone Armor Gu (Rank 2, Strength, Defensive)
- Armor from your own bones

Bone Blade Gu (Rank 2, Strength, Offensive)
- Grow bone weapons

Skeletal Reinforcement Gu (Rank 3, Strength, Utility)
- Permanent body enhancement

Bone Puppet Gu (Rank 3, Enslavement, Utility)
- Control bone constructs

White Bone Transformation Gu (Rank 4, Transformation, Utility)
- Transform into bone creature
```

**Clan Bonuses:**
- +15% Strength Path attainment gain
- Combat damage +10%
- Starting health +20

**Clan Politics:**
- Aggressive expansion stance
- Enemies with Gu Yue Clan
- Allies with beast hunters

**Unique Killer Move (Learnable):**
```
Bone Storm
Requires: Master Strength Path, 2 bone Gu
Effect: AoE damage, armor shred
```

- Age: 16
- Resources: 75 primeval stones
- Starting Gu: Suggestion Gu (Rank 1)

**Unique Gu Pool:**

Soul Contract Gu (Rank 2, Enslavement, Utility)

Puppet Thread Gu (Rank 2, Enslavement, Offensive)
- Control enemy temporarily

Information Network Gu (Rank 3, Wisdom, Utility)
- Spy network benefits

Shadow Servant Gu (Rank 3, Enslavement, Utility)
- Invisible helper

Master's Voice Gu (Rank 4, Enslavement, Utility)

Absolute Dominion

## Clan Mechanics

### Changing Clans

Possible but difficult:
- Leave current clan (reputation hit)
- Prove worth to new clan
- Start as Outsider/Probationary
- Lose access to old clan Gu

### Clan Destruction

If your clan is destroyed:
- Become clanless
- Lose clan benefits
- Can join another or stay independent
- Some unique events available

### Clan Missions

Each clan has mission pool:

| Mission Type | Frequency | Reward Range |
|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| Patrol | Always | 10-30 stones |
| Gathering | Always | 15-40 stones |
| Investigation | Sometimes | 20-50 stones |
| Combat | Sometimes | 30-80 stones |
| Special | Rare | 50-200 stones |

## AI Generation for Clans

When generating clan-specific content:

### Gu Generation
- Must fit clan's signature paths
- Names should reflect clan themes
- Effects should complement clan playstyle

### Event Generation
- Reference clan politics
- Include clan NPCs
- Respect clan standing effects

### NPC Generation
- Clan members have clan-appropriate Gu
- Personalities reflect clan culture
- Relationships follow clan politics
