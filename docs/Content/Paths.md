# Cultivation Paths [DRAFT]

## Overview

There are 10 main cultivation paths, each with distinct themes, mechanics, and playstyles. Players can focus on one path or dabble in multiple.

---

## Path Mechanics

### Path Attainment

Each path has an attainment level representing mastery:

| Level | Points | Bonus | Unlocks |
|-------|--------|-------|---------|
| Novice | 0-99 | None | Basic Gu usage |
| Adept | 100-299 | +10% efficiency | Path synergies |
| Expert | 300-599 | +20% efficiency | Advanced techniques |
| Master | 600-999 | +30% efficiency | Killer moves |
| Grandmaster | 1000+ | +40% efficiency | Signature moves |

### Gaining Attainment

- **Study:** Dedicated study sessions
- **Use:** Using path Gu in combat/exploration
- **Experimentation:** Trying new combinations
- **Teaching:** Learning from NPCs
- **Inheritance:** Prior run knowledge

### Path Affinity

Some characters have natural affinity:
- Clan determines starting affinity
- Aptitude affects learning speed
- Multiple paths possible but slower

## The 10 Main Paths

### 1. Strength Path

**Theme:** Physical power, body cultivation, direct force

**Aesthetic:**
- Muscles, bones, physical might
- Impact, force, endurance
- Martial arts, body refinement

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Highest single-target damage
- Self-buff focus
- High health/defense options
- Simple but powerful effects

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Fist techniques** - Bonus damage with consecutive attacks
- **Body hardening** - Damage reduction scales with health
- **Overwhelming power** - Ignore portion of enemy defense

**Combat Style:**
- Aggressive, straightforward
- Win through attrition and damage
- Weak to control and kiting

**Example Gu:**
```
Iron Fist Gu (Rank 2, Offensive)
Cost: 20 essence
Deal 40 damage. If this is your third attack this turn, deal 60 instead.

Steel Body Gu (Rank 3, Defensive)
Cost: 25 essence
Gain 20% damage reduction for 3 turns. While active, strength attacks deal +15%.
```

---

### 2. Wisdom Path

**Theme:** Knowledge, deduction, information manipulation

**Aesthetic:**
- Books, scrolls, mental imagery
- Light, clarity, calculation
- Chess, strategy, planning

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Information advantage
- Card draw and selection
- Predict and counter enemies
- Support other paths

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Deduction** - Reveal enemy intentions
- **Calculation** - Reduce costs with setup
- **Memory** - Store and replay effects

**Combat Style:**
- Reactive, adaptive
- Win through information advantage
- Weak to aggression before setup

**Example Gu:**
```
Insight Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 15 essence
Reveal 2 random Gu in enemy's loadout. Draw a card.

Calculated Strike Gu (Rank 3, Offensive)
Cost: 30 essence
Deal damage equal to 10× the number of enemy Gu you've seen this combat.
```

---

### 3. Blood Path

**Theme:** Life force, sacrifice, vitality manipulation

**Aesthetic:**
- Blood, wounds, hearts
- Red, crimson, pulsing
- Sacrifice, vampirism

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Life manipulation (self and enemy)
- Sacrifice health for power
- Healing and life steal
- Debuffs through blood

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Blood sacrifice** - Pay health for enhanced effects
- **Life steal** - Heal from damage dealt
- **Blood bond** - Link health pools

**Combat Style:**
- Risk/reward gambling
- Sustain through life steal
- Weak when burst down before sustain kicks in

**Example Gu:**
```
Blood Frenzy Gu (Rank 2, Offensive)
Cost: 10 essence + 15 health
Deal 45 damage. If you're below 50% health, deal 65 instead.

Blood Siphon Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 20 essence
Your attacks heal you for 30% of damage dealt for 2 turns.
```

---

### 4. Refinement Path

**Theme:** Gu creation, modification, meta-mechanics

**Aesthetic:**
- Forges, cauldrons, tools
- Transformation, crafting
- Runes, formulas

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Modify other Gu
- Create temporary Gu
- Enhance allies, weaken enemies
- Economic advantages

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Transmutation** - Change Gu properties
- **Fusion** - Combine Gu temporarily
- **Deconstruction** - Break down enemy Gu

**Combat Style:**
- Flexible, toolbox approach
- Win through adaptation
- Weak to straightforward aggression

**Example Gu:**
```
Enhance Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 15 essence
Target Gu costs 5 less and deals 20% more damage this combat.

Forge Blade Gu (Rank 3, Utility)
Cost: 25 essence
Create a temporary Rank 2 offensive Gu in your hand. Lasts this combat.
```

---

### 5. Transformation Path

**Theme:** Shapeshifting, adaptation, change

**Aesthetic:**
- Morphing, fluidity
- Multiple forms, chimeras
- Flexibility, deception

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Change forms for different abilities
- Adapt to enemy weaknesses
- Copy enemy Gu
- Versatile but master of none

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Shapeshift** - Access different ability sets
- **Mimic** - Copy enemy abilities
- **Hybrid forms** - Combine benefits

**Combat Style:**
- Reactive adaptation
- Win through flexibility
- Weak to enemies that can't be countered by adaptation

**Example Gu:**
```
Wolf Form Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 20 essence
Transform into wolf form for 3 turns. Gain +20 speed, attacks cause bleed.

Mirror Gu (Rank 3, Utility)
Cost: 30 essence
Copy target enemy's last used Gu ability.
```

---

### 6. Enslavement Path

**Theme:** Control, domination, summoning

**Aesthetic:**
- Chains, collars, contracts
- Beasts, minions, puppets
- Willpower, domination

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Summon creatures to fight
- Control enemy actions
- Steal enemy resources
- Action economy advantage

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Summon** - Add allies to combat
- **Dominate** - Control enemy temporarily
- **Steal** - Take enemy Gu or resources

**Combat Style:**
- Build board presence
- Win through numbers
- Weak to AoE and single-target burst (kill master)

**Example Gu:**
```
Beast Contract Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 25 essence
Summon a Rank 1 beast that attacks for 15 damage each turn for 3 turns.

Mind Shackle Gu (Rank 3, Utility)
Cost: 35 essence
Enemy's next action targets themselves or an ally instead.
```

---

### 7. Poison Path

**Theme:** Toxins, corruption, slow death

**Aesthetic:**
- Venom, acid, decay
- Green, purple, sickly colors
- Insects, snakes, rot

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Damage over time
- Stacking debuffs
- Area denial
- Inevitability

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Poison stacks** - Damage scales with stacks
- **Spread** - Poison jumps between targets
- **Toxic environment** - Damage zones

**Combat Style:**
- Patient, wearing down
- Win through attrition
- Weak to cleanse effects and burst damage

**Example Gu:**
```
Venom Strike Gu (Rank 2, Offensive)
Cost: 15 essence
Deal 10 damage. Apply 2 poison stacks (5 damage per stack per turn, 4 turns).

Plague Cloud Gu (Rank 3, Offensive)
Cost: 30 essence
All enemies take 20 damage and gain 3 poison stacks.
```

---

### 8. Dark Path (Shadow/Stealth)

**Theme:** Shadows, stealth, assassination

**Aesthetic:**
- Darkness, shadows, night
- Hidden blades, smoke
- Surprise, ambush

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Stealth and surprise attacks
- Extra damage from hidden
- Evasion and misdirection
- Burst damage windows

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Stealth** - Can't be targeted until revealed
- **Ambush** - Bonus damage when attacking from stealth
- **Shadow step** - Reposition and evade

**Combat Style:**
- Burst windows from stealth
- Win through assassination
- Weak to AoE and detection

**Example Gu:**
```
Shadow Cloak Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 20 essence
Enter stealth for 2 turns. Your next attack deals +30 damage.

Assassinate Gu (Rank 3, Offensive)
Cost: 35 essence
Deal 30 damage. If target is below 30% health, deal 90 instead.
```

---

### 9. Light Path

**Theme:** Radiance, protection, revelation

**Aesthetic:**
- Light, sun, moon
- Shields, barriers, halos
- Purity, clarity, truth

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Strong defense and shielding
- Cleanse negative effects
- Reveal hidden enemies
- Healing support

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Shield** - Absorb damage
- **Cleanse** - Remove debuffs
- **Illuminate** - Reveal and disable stealth/darkness

**Combat Style:**
- Defensive, outlasting
- Win through attrition and defense
- Weak to sustained damage that overwhelms shields

**Example Gu:**
```
Light Shield Gu (Rank 2, Defensive)
Cost: 20 essence
Gain a shield that absorbs 40 damage for 3 turns.

Purifying Light Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 15 essence
Remove all debuffs from yourself. Heal 10 per debuff removed.
```

---

### 10. Food Path

**Theme:** Consumption, nourishment, economics

**Aesthetic:**
- Cooking, feasts, gluttony
- Growth, sustenance
- Resource generation

**Mechanical Identity:**
- Resource generation
- Sustain and recovery
- Buff through consumption
- Economic advantages outside combat

**Signature Mechanics:**
- **Consume** - Eat something for benefits
- **Digest** - Turn enemies into resources
- **Feast** - Major buff from preparation

**Combat Style:**
- Sustain and resource advantage
- Win through outlasting
- Weak to burst before sustain matters

**Example Gu:**
```
Devouring Maw Gu (Rank 2, Offensive)
Cost: 20 essence
Deal 25 damage. Gain 10 primeval stones if this kills.

Nourishing Aura Gu (Rank 2, Utility)
Cost: 15 essence
Regenerate 5% max health per turn for 4 turns. Essence regeneration +20%.
```

## Path Interactions

### Synergies

| Path A | Path B | Synergy |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Strength | Blood | Sacrifice health, hit harder |
| Wisdom | Refinement | Calculate optimal crafting |
| Poison | Dark | Poison from stealth, escape |
| Light | Blood | Shield while sacrificing |
| Enslavement | Transformation | Summon then copy summons |

### Conflicts

| Path A | Path B | Conflict |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Light | Dark | Mutual cancellation |
| Blood | Light | Sacrifice vs purity themes |
| Poison | Food | Corruption vs nourishment |

## AI Generation Guidelines

When generating path-specific Gu:

### Required Themes
Each path MUST include its core themes:
- Use path-appropriate vocabulary
- Effects should match path identity
- Visual descriptions fit aesthetic

### Mechanical Boundaries
Stay within path's mechanical identity:
- Strength shouldn't have complex setup
- Wisdom shouldn't have raw damage focus
- Each path has strengths AND weaknesses

### Balance Across Paths
- No path should dominate
- Each path should have viable builds
- Cross-path combinations should be interesting but not required
